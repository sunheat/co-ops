`SGX-IX-FUT-2406`: the ACFS aggregate exceeds the SGX published aggregate by
20 contracts.

Client-level positions roll up exactly to the venue aggregate after any
adjustment.

## Actual Behavior

but the corresponding venue submission was not updated, so the aggregates
diverge.

## Investigation Notes

- The adjustment record lacks the required audit trail entry; escalated to
  operations analysts per the reconciliation-break runbook.
- Correction path agreed: submit the adjustment to SGX and rerun
  reconciliation once the venue aggregate reflects it.
