## Symptom

SGX aggregate margin does not match the client margin reconstructed from
ACFS data for client ACME-102 on business date 2024-03-15.

## Expected Behavior

The initial margin for ACME-102 reconstructed from `positions` for
`MR-20240315-SGX` equals the figure implied by the SGX aggregate, within
venue tolerance.

## Actual Behavior

`margin_results` for `MR-20240315-SGX` shows initial margin 288.00 SGD for
ACME-102, while the SGX aggregate implies 320.00 SGD for the same
positions. Variation margin (−50.00 SGD) matches on both sides.

## Investigation Notes

- ACME-102 is a `PORTFOLIO` margin-model account; the 0.90 diversification
  factor explains 320.00 × 0.90 = 288.00. Checking whether the venue
  aggregate assumes STANDARD margining.
- Position snapshot for 2024-03-15 verified against `trades` batch
  `IMP-20240315-SGX`; no late imports found.
- Awaiting venue confirmation of which margining basis the aggregate uses.
