# TKT-2024-006 — Margin run overrun blocks reconciliation

## Symptom

`margin_run` scheduled at 02:10 was still running at 03:00, past its slot;
`reconciliation` could not start and morning reporting was at risk.

## Expected Behavior

`margin_run` completes within its slot and reconciliation starts on time.

## Actual Behavior

The run overran due to an unusually large position snapshot after a backlog
reprocessing; the scheduler held reconciliation in `PENDING`.

## Investigation Notes

- Confirmed the run was making progress and allowed it to finish rather
  than stopping it, per the batch-job-failure runbook.
- Reconciliation started at 03:55 and the window was recovered by 04:40.
- Follow-up: review slot sizing after backlog reprocessing events.
